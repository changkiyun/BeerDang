# Calculator

Mode: Operate. Audience: a shopper checking one beer pack on a phone. Task: select a preset or enter a custom count and volume, enter pack price, read 10ml unit price immediately.

## Direction contract

- THESIS: Start with the calculator; no landing hero, brand illustration, English slogans, or decorative badges.
- OWN-WORLD: A compact, neutral utility with legible dark text, quiet borders, and one functional highlight for selected choices and the computed price.
- STORY: Select cans and volume, type the full pack price, see the unit price update without a button.
- FIRST VIEWPORT: A short title followed by the three controls and their result in one phone view; on desktop the form and result sit side by side.
- FORM: User-pinned function-first direction; the concept seed b59f8eec yielded a ticket-wallet motif, declined because it conflicts with the user's explicit no-branding brief. The signature interaction is the visibly updating normalized unit price.
- FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
